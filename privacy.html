Privacy

How enquiry data is handled.

Torimbia Ltd is a UK company. This page covers the contact form on torimbia.co.uk. The site does not use analytics cookies.

What I collect

Name, email, optional phone number, what you say you need, and your message. That is used only to reply to the enquiry and, if we work together, to continue the conversation.

Who processes it

Form submissions go through Formspree so they can be delivered to me.

How long I keep it

For as long as needed to handle the enquiry, and longer only if we agree to work together or I must keep a record for legal reasons. I do not sell your details or pass them on for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what I hold, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, via the contact form.
