Questions

The practical bits.

How do you charge?

Usually a scoped piece of work, sometimes a short paid discovery first so neither of us is guessing. You get a clear figure before anything starts — not a surprise at the end.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. That is a normal working day, not a workaround. I am UK-based and happy on a call wherever you are.

Can you do iOS and Android?

Yes — including store listing, review, permissions and updates, not just the build. I will be honest about what the stores will and will not allow.

Who will I actually work with?

Me. If the job needs design, extra hands or a specialist, I bring in people I already trust. You will not be handed to a bench of people you have never met.

Custom, or something off the shelf?

Whichever gets you the outcome with less risk. I will say if a standard product will do. If it will not, we build the smallest thing that solves the real problem.

How quickly can we start?

Tell me what you are stuck on. If I am a fit, we can usually talk within a few days. If I am not, I will say so.
